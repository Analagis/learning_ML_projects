Project focused on generative deep learning with Variational Autoencoders (VAE) and Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) for MNIST digit generation, latent space analysis, and controlled conditional generation.

* Derived and explained the ELBO objective for Variational Autoencoders
* Explained generator and discriminator loss functions in GANs
* Trained a VAE on MNIST with a 2-dimensional latent space
* Visualized latent representations and generated digits from a 2D latent grid
* Implemented and trained a supervised VAE with label-conditioned decoding
* Implemented and trained a GAN for handwritten digit generation
* Visualized generator and discriminator loss dynamics during GAN training
* Generated digit grids from a 2D GAN latent space
* Explained and applied the Gradient Reversal Layer in adversarial training
* Implemented a supervised / conditional GAN for controllable digit generation
